## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
```
TrainJatri - Your Complete Bangladesh Railway Guide

Find train schedules, track trains in real-time, and get comprehensive station information for all major routes across Bangladesh.

✨ FEATURES:

🚂 Complete Train Schedules
Access detailed schedules for all trains with arrival and departure times for every station along the route.

🏛️ Station Information
View all trains departing from major railway stations across Bangladesh. Search by station to plan your journey.

📍 Live Train Tracking
Track trains in real-time via SMS integration with Bangladesh Railway's official tracking service.

🌐 Bilingual Support
Browse seamlessly in both English and Bengali with full translation support.

⭐ Quick Access
Save your favorite trains, stations, and routes for instant access. No need to search repeatedly.

📴 Offline Ready
Access train schedules without internet connection. All schedule data is available offline.

🌙 Dark Mode
Comfortable viewing experience in any lighting condition with automatic dark mode support.

🎯 PERFECT FOR:
• Daily commuters planning their work trips
• Travelers exploring beautiful Bangladesh
• Students and professionals using railway regularly
• Anyone planning railway journeys

🗺️ COVERAGE:
All major routes and stations including:
• Dhaka - Chattogram
• Dhaka - Sylhet
• Dhaka - Rajshahi
• Dhaka - Khulna
• Dhaka - Rangpur
• And many more intercity and mail/express trains

📱 EASY TO USE:
Simply search for your train by name, browse stations, or track live locations. The intuitive interface makes journey planning effortless.

🔒 PRIVACY:
Your data stays on your device. We don't collect or share personal information.

Download TrainJatri today and make your railway journey planning effortless!

For support or feedback: feedback@trainjatri.com
Website: trainjatri.com
```

## Privacy Policy (Simple Template)

```
Privacy Policy for TrainJatri

Last updated: [Current Date]

INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE
TrainJatri does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All train schedules and saved preferences are stored locally on your device.

DATA STORAGE
Any data you save in the app (favorite trains, stations, routes) is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

SMS FUNCTIONALITY
The live tracking feature uses Bangladesh Railway's official SMS service (16318). When you use this feature, the app opens your device's SMS app to send a tracking request. We do not access, store, or transmit your SMS messages.
```
